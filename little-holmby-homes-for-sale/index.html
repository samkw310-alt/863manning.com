Little Holmby homes for sale

Little Holmby is a small, mostly single-family neighborhood in Westwood on LA's Westside, between UCLA and Holmby Hills. Homes here don't come up often. Here's a quick guide for buyers, plus a home that's available now.

Where is Little Holmby?

Little Holmby sits in Westwood, in the 90024 zip code. It's generally described as bounded by Sunset Blvd, Beverly Glen Blvd, Santa Monica Blvd and the 405 freeway. Holmby Hills and Bel Air are to the north, and Century City and Beverly Hills are a short drive east.

What's nearby

  • UCLA and the UCLA medical campus
  • Westwood Village shops, restaurants and theaters
  • Century City offices and shopping
  • Beverly Hills, Holmby Hills and Bel Air
  • Quick access to the 405 and Wilshire Blvd

What homes are like

The neighborhood is largely single-family residential, with tree-lined streets and a mix of older homes and newer rebuilds. Styles vary from street to street. One-story homes and homes with a guest house are less common, which is part of what makes 863 Manning Ave stand out.

What to check before you buy in Little Holmby

  • Permits and zoning. Confirm the permitted status of any additions or guest house with the City of Los Angeles (LADBS).
  • Inspections. Many homes here are decades old. Plan for a general home inspection plus sewer line, roof and foundation checks.
  • Schools. Confirm school assignments directly with LAUSD for the specific address.
  • Commute. Test the drive to work at rush hour; the 405 and Wilshire corridor can be busy.
  • Insurance. Get homeowners insurance quotes early; availability and cost in Los Angeles have changed in recent years.
