The home

A classic Little Holmby traditional, with a flexible path to ownership

Set behind manicured hedges and bougainvillea on a quiet, tree-lined street, 863 Manning Ave is a two-story traditional with four bedrooms, two full baths and a powder room, hardwood floors, two fireplaces, central air and an updated kitchen and baths. A detached guest house opens onto the lawn through French doors, and a private rear patio is made for outdoor dining. Independently appraised at $4,650,000 (July 2026) and offered at $4,600,000, with the seller open to a lease option, seller carryback and other creative terms.

Financing & terms

Buy it your way: lease option, seller financing or creative terms

Not every buyer fits a standard jumbo loan. The seller will consider structures that get the right buyer in the door.

Lease option to buy

Lease-to-own / rent-to-own: move in now, lock in the purchase price, and buy later. Option fee, rent credits and term negotiable.

Seller may carry

Seller financing / owner financing through a seller carryback note with an acceptable down payment. Rate and term negotiable.

Lease-purchase

A committed purchase on a set future date, with the lease period giving you time to sell, close a loan or arrange funds.

Hybrid & creative

Conventional or jumbo first with a seller second, 1031 exchange buyers, and other creative financing proposals are welcome.

All terms are subject to seller approval and a written agreement. Nothing on this page is a commitment to lend or an offer of credit. Buyers should consult their own attorney, tax and lending advisors.

Is a lease option or rent-to-own available?

Yes. The seller will consider a lease option to buy (lease-to-own / rent-to-own) or a lease-purchase. Option fee, monthly rent, rent credits and term are negotiable.

Will the seller carry financing?

The seller may carry. Seller financing / owner financing via a seller carryback note will be considered with an acceptable down payment.

What other creative financing is considered?
