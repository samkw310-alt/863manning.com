Overview

Set behind manicured hedges and bougainvillea on a quiet, tree-lined street in Little Holmby, 863 Manning Ave is a 1949 two-story traditional with four bedrooms, two full baths and a powder room. It has hardwood floors, two fireplaces, central air, and an updated kitchen and baths. A detached guest house opens onto the lawn through French doors, and there's a private rear patio for outdoor dining.

The home is vacant and available now. It was independently appraised at $4,650,000 (July 2026) and is offered at $4,600,000. The seller is open to a lease option, seller carryback financing and other creative terms.
